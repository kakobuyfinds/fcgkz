What to check before ordering

Match the listing and variant

Open the product page and locate the original seller link. Save the seller URL, chosen size and color, and current price. Check that your agent accepts the link and shows the same variant before paying.

Compare measurements and QC photos

Compare the seller’s size chart with an item you own. If warehouse quality-control photos are available, check the size label, color and stitching. Ask for an insole or garment measurement if the fit is uncertain.

Compare shipping quotes

Use your destination and the packed parcel dimensions when getting quotes. Check whether the carrier charges by actual or volumetric weight, and confirm which taxes and handling fees the quote includes.

Estimate your order total

Convert all amounts to the same currency first. Include only taxes and fees that are outside the shipping quote.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

A HooBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list that helps shoppers find items, compare listings and save links for a shopping agent. This independent guide uses listings from HipobuyFind. Check agent compatibility with the original seller link.

Does the catalog price include international shipping?

The amount shown is the item’s catalog price. Get a parcel quote for your destination and add any domestic delivery, service fees and applicable taxes to estimate the full cost.

Can I use another buyer’s QC photos to confirm my size?

Use those photos to see what another buyer received. For your own order, compare the warehouse photos with your saved variant and request measurements before approving shipment if you need to verify the fit.
